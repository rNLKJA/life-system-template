# Session modes

Read the section for the mode you are running. Storage rules (the dated file, the `MEMORY.md` row and the queue rewrite) are in `SKILL.md`, under "Research and storage".

## Deep scan

Weekly, or on request for a market read.

```
Deep scan progress:
- [ ] Read last week's scan first, so this one reports what shifted, not what stayed
- [ ] Work through references/sources.md with three to five searches, plus the radar searches (references/strategic-work.md)
- [ ] Synthesise across sources rather than reporting each one
- [ ] Run the effort check and the strategic-twin pass for the week
- [ ] Pick one recommendation from the radar, the twins or the market read: whichever compounds most
- [ ] Store it: dated scan file, MEMORY.md row, queue rewrite
```

Output:

```
SCAN, <date>
Top signal: the most important thing the market is doing that affects the owner
Frontier: the skill, tool or idea gaining traction that the owner should know
Radar: the best live opportunity this week, with its link, its score on the five checks and the proof to lead with
Positioning gap: one honest gap between where the owner is and where the market is going
Effort check: strategic vs operational this week, and the one operational load to cut, delegate or automate
Strategic twin: this week's operational work that could compound, and the smallest step that makes it public
Recommendation: one specific action, not a category
```

## Full session

On request: direction, project strategy, an accountability review, life planning.

```
Full session progress:
- [ ] Effort read: where recent effort actually went (strategic vs operational), against the stated priorities; flag drift before diagnosing
- [ ] Strategic queue: what moved, what stalled and why
- [ ] Ask two or three targeted questions before advising; diagnose before prescribing
- [ ] What is working, what is not, what the market says
- [ ] One life check from the rotation (SKILL.md, "Life module")
- [ ] End with exactly one recommended action and one accountability question
- [ ] Store it: dated session file, MEMORY.md row, queue rewrite
```

## One-liner

For Donna's daily brief, or when the owner asks for a single growth line: one market or skill signal, or one life nudge, in one sentence, from one search at most. When the week has tilted operational, make it a drift nudge that points at the top item in the strategic queue, with its size in minutes.
